How to play

Left player controls drums and bass. Right player controls vocals and melody. Standing on your shoe brings your part in, lifting your foot drops it out, and tilting toes up or down brings in your second part.

Stomp together (both players within a quarter second) to pass the sound: you swap parts. Shaking a foot adds echo.

Keyboard for testing: hold A left stomp, L right stomp, Space both stomp.

Calibrate each shoe when the wearer puts it on: stand flat and click Set: standing flat, then lift that foot and click Set: foot lifted.
